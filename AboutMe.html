Hobbies and Interests:

I enjoy attending game or computer science related conferences and talks, as I love to learn about new ideas which provide me with the knowledge to improve my own projects.

I love video games that give me a wide set of complex options so that I can optimise my playstyle and challenge myself by enforcing difficult rules on how I play the game. This is why my favourite game is Divinity Original Sin II, alongside it's deep story, rich environments, and intricate combat system, it allows for such a wide diversity of play that after several hundred hours of play, I am still discovering new ways to optimise and enjoy it each time I play.


I’m an avid fan of table top games and regularly meet up with a group of friends to play new and old beloved games alike. I’ve recently been getting into LUA scripting to be able to make mods for the game Tabletop Simulator where we meet up weekly for board games or sessions of my year-long DND campaign.

Baking is great fun, and I love sharing my baked goods, especially my famous cookies with my mates.

I’ve played for a regional level basketball team which has positively influenced my communication and teamwork and I still play to keep healthy and happy.

I also enjoy reading fantasy.
